# Pet Cat

Murchyk the cat lives on your home screen. He gets hungry and bored as real time passes
and sleeps at night (23:00–7:00). Feeding or playing at night wakes him up for a while.

## Install

Copy `wallpaper.lua` to the SD card as `/sd/wallpaper.lua`. To feed, play with or pet him,
open `/sd/wallpaper.lua` from the file manager. His state is saved in `pet.txt`.

## Controls (app only)

- **A** — Feed.
- **B** — Play.
- **C** — Pet (stroke) — he purrs, a bit more joy.
- **START** — Exit.
