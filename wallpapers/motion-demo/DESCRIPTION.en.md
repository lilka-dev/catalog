# Motion Demo

A demoscene reel: ten animated scenes take turns every 9 seconds,
with blinds and squares transitions between them.

## Scenes

- **Plasma**
- **Tunnel**
- **Morph** — sphere, torus, cube, knot, helix.
- **Squash & Stretch** — bouncing cube.
- **Twister**
- **Copper & Scroll** — bars, logo, sine scroller.
- **Easing** — six easing functions.
- **Fireworks** — over a city.
- **Waves** — 3D wireframe surface.
- **Harmonograph**

## Install

Copy `wallpaper.lua` to the SD card as `/sd/wallpaper.lua`. The launcher shows it on the home screen.

## Controls (app only)

- **← →** — Previous / next scene.
- **A** — Pause scene switching.
- **START** — Exit.
