# Kyiv Metro

A schematic map of the Kyiv metro with trains running on all three lines.
Trains come more often at rush hour, and at night the metro is closed.
Every few seconds a random station is highlighted with its name.

## Install

Copy `wallpaper.lua` to the SD card as `/sd/wallpaper.lua`.
Opening hours are in `OPEN` and `CLOSE` at the top of the script.

## Controls (app only)

- **Left / Right** — Previous / next station.
- **START** — Exit.
